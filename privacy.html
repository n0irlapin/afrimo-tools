Privacy Policy — Afrimo View Tracker

Afrimo View Tracker is an internal reporting tool for the AFRIMO brand's own TikTok account. It is not used by the general public.

Data we access

With the account owner's authorization: basic profile info (open id, display name, avatar) and the account's public video list with statistics (title, cover image, view, like, comment and share counts).

How the data is used

The data is stored only on the marketing team's own computer to show daily view count changes in an internal dashboard. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising targeting.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed for internal reporting and can be deleted at any time by removing the local data files. Authorization can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app settings.
