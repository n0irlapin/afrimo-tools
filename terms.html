Terms of Service — Afrimo View Tracker

Afrimo View Tracker is an internal tool used only by the AFRIMO brand marketing team to check view counts of videos posted on the brand's own TikTok account.

The tool is not offered to the public. Only the brand's own TikTok account is connected, and only with the account owner's authorization through TikTok Login Kit.

The tool reads basic profile information and the list of the account's public videos (title, cover image, view, like, comment and share counts). It does not post, edit or delete any content.

Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app settings.
